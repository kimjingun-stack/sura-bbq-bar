Chapter 01 — Our Name

SURA is the Korean word for the royal meal once served to Korean kings. It carries the spirit of care, dignity, and sincere hospitality — the way every guest deserves to be treated.

Built on years of restaurant experience across Korea, China, and now Indonesia — from Shanghai, Suzhou, Nanjing, Changzhou and Wuxi since 2008, to today in Jakarta.

A royal meal,
served to every guest.

SURA reinterprets the spirit of Korean royal dining for the modern Jakarta lifestyle — premium without distance, Korean without rigidity, modern without losing warmth. Every meal is prepared with the belief that good food should make people feel welcomed, satisfied, and remembered.

The Taste of Happiness, Crafted with Care.
