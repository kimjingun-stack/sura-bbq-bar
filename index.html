03BRAND STORY

From the Royal Table to Yours

SURA takes its name from surasang, the royal table of Korea's kings — where the finest ingredients were prepared with the greatest care. That is the spirit we bring to every table today.

Our Tteokgalbi, once served to royalty, is made from finely minced beef, seasoned with Korean soy sauce and grilled to order. Beside it, our 100% Wagyu Hamburg Steak carries the same tradition into a modern form. SURA is where Korea's culinary heritage meets contemporary dining.

What Makes SURA Special

Live Kitchen

From preparation to grilling, torching and plating, guests watch the entire process unfold in front of them. We designed the making of every meal as a performance — and those are the moments guests want to capture and share.

Ingredients

100% Wagyu beef. Japonica rice. Omega-3 eggs. No ready-made products are used in our stores — sauces, marinades, side dishes and desserts are all made by us.

Space

The elegance of Korean tradition in a modern, New York-inspired setting: exposed concrete, warm wood, stainless steel, and bar seating facing the kitchen.

How SURA Operates

Finance
  • Monthly P&L for every store, closed and reviewed with management in the first week of each month.
  • Budget vs. actual tracking per store — food cost, labor cost and discount rate monitored against targets.
  • Central purchasing: supplier negotiation, inventory control and quality monitoring for all stores and the central kitchens.
  • Internal audit of transactions and records against company policy and local regulations.
  • Daily accounting: sales reporting, payroll, tax, utilities and expense tracking for stable cash flow.
  • Shared finance dashboard for the Chairman, CEO and finance lead — decisions made on the same live numbers.
Marketing
  • Membership CRM: 50,000+ registered members. AI segments guests by visit timing and frequency and sends personalized offers — a welcome-back message to a lapsed guest, a reward to a frequent one — at the right moment.
  • Social reach: Instagram 16K followers (Jakarta + Surabaya) and TikTok 19K followers, with TikTok 6–7 posts and Instagram Reels 4–5 posts per week.
  • Creator program: influencers scored on reach, engagement and brand fit, with a tiered approval process before any contract.
  • Store openings run on a 4-phase launch playbook: awareness → anticipation → tasting panel & creators → grand opening and UGC.
  • Local brand collaborations and offline events in every city (Spikoe, Sambal Bu Rudy in Surabaya).
  • Weekly performance review in a Continue / Stop / Test / Fix format — every campaign is measured, not just produced.
Human Resources
  • Onboarding: every hire learns the service SOP in the first week, followed by 2 days of on-the-job training.
  • 30-day probation review on a checklist, quarterly refresher training, and a daily morning briefing in every store.
  • Right person, right place: store managers assign positions by temperament — outgoing staff to the floor, meticulous staff to the steak station.
  • Career path from Crew to Section Leader, Shift Manager, Assistant Manager and Store Manager — all five store managers were promoted from within.
  • A transparent reward and discipline system with clear warning stages, applied the same way in every store.
  • Multilingual team (Korean, Chinese, Indonesian, English) connected through in-house translation tools.
Central Kitchen
  • Two central kitchens — Jakarta and Surabaya — each supplying its city's stores daily.
  • No ready-made products: six signature sauces, marinades, kimchi, pickles, side dishes and desserts are all made in-house.
  • Standardized recipes and batch records, so every store receives the same product every day.
  • A different traditional Korean soup every week, rotated across all stores.
  • New menu development pipeline: ingredient prep → test cooking → scoring → CEO tasting → final recipe card in three languages for every store.
  • Ingredient sourcing, hygiene and food-safety checks managed at the central kitchen, not left to each store.
Fitout
  • A 100-day opening checklist and a construction progress checklist, used on all five openings.
  • Every task is scheduled backwards from the soft-opening date; overdue items are flagged as urgent automatically.
  • Daily site reports with photos in the Manager app, reviewed by HQ the same day.
  • Readiness tracked by category: construction & facilities, delivery & purchasing, central kitchen & tasting, store operations.
  • Mall compliance handled with experience — sprinkler and staircase approvals, exhaust pressure, duct leakage tests.
  • Ongoing maintenance and inspections for all existing stores.

Quality Control
  • Every dish follows a written recipe with portion weights and cooking times — no variation by store or by chef.
  • Steaks are served in two courses so each piece reaches the guest at its best temperature.
  • Chefs complete training and a practical certification before grilling in front of guests; refreshers every quarter.
  • Daily hygiene and food-safety checklists in every store and both central kitchens.
  • Semi-annual mystery shopper visits scored against a 100-point standard (target ≥ 90).
Service Standard
"SURA hall staff are not servers of food — they are friends who gift each guest a moment of happiness."
  • Step 1 · Greeting (0–30 seconds): bright smile, eye contact, open-palm gesture and a light small talk — lift the guest's mood in the first half minute.
  • Step 2 · Ordering (within 2 minutes): guide the QR ordering, mention running events, and have the top-3 recommendations ready.
  • Step 3 · Serving: every dish arrives with "Please enjoy" and a one-line brand story — the Japonica rice, this week's traditional soup, 100% Wagyu grilled in our kitchen.
  • Step 4 · Active Watching: a visual check of every table every 5–10 minutes; offer rice, soup or corn tea refills before the guest asks.
  • Step 5 · Feedback Check: "How was the food?" — praise gets a brand-story reply; a complaint follows the HEART protocol (Hear, Empathize, Apologize, Resolve, Thank) with escalation to the store manager when needed.
  • Step 6 · Farewell: hand over the stamp voucher or VIP card, invite a Google or Instagram review, and say goodbye at the door with eye contact.
  • Regular guests: staff remember three things — name, usual order and visit pattern — and greet returning guests accordingly.
  • Red flags that are never allowed: personal questions, one-word answers, mentioning competitors, crossed arms or checking a phone while serving.
  • Trained and measured: SOP learning in week one, 30-day review, quarterly refresher, daily briefing, semi-annual mystery shopper. KPIs: Google rating ≥ 4.6, repeat-visit rate ≥ 30%, "service" mentioned in ≥ 40% of positive reviews.
Daily Feedback Loop
  • Reviews are collected daily from Google, Instagram and TikTok, and in-store from guests directly.
  • The store manager reports the day's feedback to the general manager the same day; fixes are assigned immediately.
  • The Manager app's AI summarizes each store's reviews daily, weekly and monthly — recurring themes become action items.
  • Every Google review receives a reply; complaints follow the HEART protocol with defined severity levels and compensation.
  • Store-level KPIs reviewed monthly: Google rating ≥ 4.6, repeat-visit rate ≥ 30%, service mentioned in ≥ 40% of positive reviews.
